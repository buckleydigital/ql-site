How it's calculated

Three numbers. One truth.

01

Cost per lead

Ad spend divided by leads generated. This is what you pay to get someone to raise their hand and express interest.

02

Jobs won

Leads multiplied by your close rate. Not every lead becomes a job - your close rate determines how many do.

03

CAC (your number)

Ad spend divided by jobs won. This is the true marketing cost per customer - what every job actually costs you to acquire.

The formula:  CAC = Monthly Ad Spend ÷ (Leads × Close Rate)   ·   ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend   ·   This calculator only includes paid ad spend - not platform fees, tools, or your time.
