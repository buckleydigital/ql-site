The offer
We'll deliver 30+ qualified enquiries in 30 days, or we keep running free until you get them.

This is a qualified enquiries guarantee, not a sales or revenue guarantee. If we don't deliver 30 qualified enquiries within the 30-day period, we'll keep managing and running your system at no management fee until you do. Available to eligible Australian installation businesses that satisfy every condition below, including funding the advertising spend we recommend (capped at $100 per day) and tracking every lead, quote and enquiry outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1.

Overview

What this Promotion is.

This Promotion is a performance-based offer made available by QuoteLeads (ABN 83 894 208 171) ("QuoteLeads") to eligible Clients under Section 17 (Promotions and Special Offers) of the QuoteLeads Branded Lead Gen System Terms (the "Principal Terms").

The offer: QuoteLeads will deliver 30 or more qualified enquiries to the Client within a 30-day period. Where 30 qualified enquiries have not been delivered within that 30-day period, QuoteLeads will continue to manage and run the Client's system at no management fee until 30 qualified enquiries have been delivered, provided the Client has satisfied the conditions in these Promotional Terms, including continuing to fund the recommended advertising spend.

Qualified enquiries, not sales. A "qualified enquiry" is an enquiry from a genuine potential customer that is delivered through the managed system and recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ, and that meets the qualification criteria agreed in writing between the parties. It is not a booked job, a quote accepted, a signed contract, revenue, profit, margin, or collected cash. Whether the Client converts, quotes, wins, or profits from an enquiry depends on the Client's own pricing, sales process, follow-up and capacity.

This is not a sales or revenue guarantee. QuoteLeads does not guarantee, and these Promotional Terms do not create any guarantee of, sales, conversion rates, jobs booked, signed contracts, revenue, cash flow, profit, or margin. The Promotion relates solely to the number of qualified enquiries delivered through the managed system.

Advertising spend. The Client funds the advertising spend at the level recommended by QuoteLeads, and QuoteLeads' recommended daily advertising spend will not exceed $100 per day. The Client's obligation is limited to that recommended spend level, and QuoteLeads will not require the Client to spend more than $100 per day on advertising to qualify for this Promotion.

Tracking from Day 1. The Client must track every lead, quote and enquiry outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1 of the management period. Activity that is not recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ does not count toward the milestone and may void the Promotion.

These Promotional Terms form part of the agreement between the parties in respect of this Promotion, in accordance with Section 17 of the Principal Terms. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Principal Terms.

Australia only. This Promotion is available solely to businesses located in Australia that meet the eligibility criteria set out in the Principal Terms and in these Promotional Terms.

Eligibility

Who can apply.

To be eligible for this Promotion, the Client must satisfy all of the following at the time of acceptance and throughout the management period:

  • Australian installation business. Located in Australia and operating as an installer in the trade categories QuoteLeads supports.
  • Has the capacity to service the enquiries. Sufficient sales and admin capacity to respond to, quote and follow up the additional enquiries generated under this Promotion within its normal lead times.
  • Can meet all six conditions. As set out in the Conditions section below.
  • Can fund the recommended advertising spend. A daily advertising budget at the level recommended by QuoteLeads, capped at $100 per day.
  • Can track everything in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1. All leads, quotes and enquiry outcomes must be recorded and kept up to date in QuoteLeadsHQ from the first day of the management period.

QuoteLeads may request reasonable evidence of capacity or budget before accepting a Client into the Promotion. QuoteLeads may decline to apply, or may withdraw, this Promotion where it reasonably believes the Client does not meet the capacity, budget or tracking requirements, or has obtained or used the Promotion fraudulently, dishonestly, or in breach of the Principal Terms, consistent with Section 17.

Conditions to apply

You must meet all six conditions.

To be eligible for this Promotion and to maintain it throughout the management period, the Client must satisfy all six of the following conditions, continuously and without exception. Failure to meet any condition voids the guarantee and releases QuoteLeads from the free-management obligation.

  • 1
    AI SMS switched on The AI SMS system must remain active and enabled throughout the entire management period. Disabling, pausing, or interfering with AI SMS at any point voids the Promotion.
  • 2
    Respond to QuoteLeads within 24 hours All communication from QuoteLeads, including campaign matters, new approval requests, and similar items, must be answered within 24 hours.
  • 3
    Work with QuoteLeads over a consecutive 30-day period The engagement must run continuously for 30 days, with ads running for the full 30 days consecutively. No pauses, no interruptions, and no gaps in campaign delivery.
  • 4
    Fund the recommended spend and number of campaigns The Client must fund the advertising spend and the number of campaigns recommended by QuoteLeads as necessary to deliver 30 qualified enquiries in 30 days. QuoteLeads' recommended daily advertising spend will not exceed $100 per day. Reducing spend or campaign volume below the recommended level, or failing to fund the budget, voids the Promotion.
  • 5
    Maintain the capacity to service enquiries The Client must maintain sufficient sales, quoting and admin capacity, and stock and installer access, to respond to and service the volume of enquiries generated under this Promotion within its normal lead times. The Client must not pause campaigns, decline enquiries, or cancel campaign delivery because it lacks capacity. The guarantee covers qualified enquiries only. It does not cover, and is not conditional on, the Client's ability to quote, convert, win, deliver, or collect.
  • 6
    Track everything in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1 All leads, quotes and enquiry outcomes must be recorded and kept up to date in QuoteLeadsHQ from the first day of the management period. No off-platform tracking and no unrecorded enquiries. If a lead, quote or enquiry outcome is not recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ, it may not count toward the milestone and may void the Promotion.
Mechanics

How the guarantee works.

30-day window. The 30-day period begins on the date the Client's ads first go live and the management engagement commences, consistent with Section 3 of the Principal Terms.

The milestone. Across that 30-day period, QuoteLeads tracks the number of qualified enquiries delivered through the managed system and recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ. The goal is to deliver 30 or more qualified enquiries within the 30-day period.

If the milestone is missed. Where 30 qualified enquiries have not been delivered and recorded within the 30-day period, QuoteLeads will continue to manage and run the Client's system at no management fee until 30 qualified enquiries have been delivered, provided the Client has satisfied all six conditions above throughout, including continuing to fund the recommended advertising spend (capped at $100 per day).

Important: This is a qualified enquiries guarantee, not a sales or revenue guarantee. The milestone is measured on the number of qualified enquiries delivered and recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ. It is not measured on quotes issued, jobs booked, contracts signed, revenue, invoiced work, collected cash or profit. If 30 qualified enquiries have not been delivered within the 30-day period, QuoteLeads will keep running the Client's system for free until they are, provided all six conditions above are satisfied. While management fees are waived during any extension, the Client continues to fund the recommended advertising spend, capped at $100 per day.

What "qualified enquiry" means. A qualified enquiry means an enquiry delivered through the managed system and recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ from a person who: (a) is located within the Client's service area; (b) is enquiring about a service the Client actually provides; (c) has provided valid contact details; and (d) is not a spam, test, duplicate, existing-customer, supplier, job-seeker, or sales enquiry, and that otherwise meets the qualification criteria agreed in writing between the parties. QuoteLeads determines whether an enquiry is qualified acting reasonably, based on the records in QuoteLeadsHQ. Enquiries generated from outside the managed system are not counted.

Recommended spend and campaigns. QuoteLeads will recommend the advertising spend and number of campaigns reasonably required to deliver 30 qualified enquiries in 30 days. QuoteLeads' recommended daily advertising spend will not exceed $100 per day. The Client must fund that recommendation throughout the period. If the Client reduces spend or campaign volume below the recommendation, or fails to fund the budget, the guarantee no longer applies.

Measurement and reporting. QuoteLeads will provide the Client with visibility of campaign performance and enquiry data through QuoteLeadsHQ. The Client is responsible for recording every lead, quote and enquiry outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ accurately and in a timely manner from Day 1. Any dispute about attribution or measurement is dealt with under Section 16 (Dispute Resolution) of the Principal Terms.

Satisfaction of the Promotion. Once 30 qualified enquiries have been delivered and recorded within the 30-day period, the Promotion is satisfied, regardless of how many of those enquiries the Client quotes, wins, delivers, or converts into revenue.

Capacity & fair use

Capacity is the Client's responsibility.

This Promotion measures qualified enquiries only. QuoteLeads does not promise sales, conversions, profit, or that the Client will be able to service the enquiries it receives. The Client acknowledges and agrees that:

  • The Client is solely responsible for having the sales, quoting, admin, crew, installer, stock and scheduling capacity to respond to and service enquiries within its normal lead times.
  • If the Client cannot service the volume of enquiries, whether due to staff shortages, stock delays, scheduling limits, or any other operational constraint, the Promotion is still deemed satisfied once 30 qualified enquiries have been delivered and recorded, and QuoteLeads has no further free-management obligation.
  • QuoteLeads is not liable for any lost revenue, margin, profit, missed quotes, lost jobs, cancellations, refunds, customer complaints, or reputational loss arising from the Client's capacity constraints, follow-up failures, pricing decisions, or conversion performance.
  • The Client remains responsible for its own sales process, quoting, pricing, contract terms, deposits, delivery, invoicing, and collections.

Fair use. The Client must not artificially inflate, fabricate, or manipulate the number of enquiries counted under this Promotion. Without limitation, this includes submitting its own enquiries, submitting enquiries for related parties, using false or invalid contact details, duplicating enquiries, repeatedly submitting test submissions, marking genuine enquiries as unqualified without reasonable basis, or recording enquiries for work that is not genuinely intended to proceed. Any such conduct voids the Promotion and releases QuoteLeads from all obligations under it.

Important: The Promotion is a qualified enquiries guarantee. It guarantees the delivery of 30+ qualified enquiries within 30 days, not sales, jobs, contracts, revenue or profit. All leads, quotes and enquiry outcomes must be tracked in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1, and the Client must fund the recommended advertising spend, capped at $100 per day.
