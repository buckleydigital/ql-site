Formula
Maximum cost per lead = (Revenue per sale − Direct costs − Other variable costs − Target profit per sale) × Lead-to-install conversion rate

Actual cost per lead = Total lead spend ÷ Leads purchased

Lead acquisition cost per installation = Total lead spend ÷ Completed installations

Set Your Price Thresholds

Three prices to know before you buy.

Target price
What you want to pay

Your preferred cost per lead to achieve your target profit per sale.

Break-even price
Profit consumed entirely

The maximum price at which acquisition costs consume all the profit available for lead buying. Not a recommended target.

Absolute ceiling
Minimum profit preserved

The highest price that still preserves your minimum required profit, with a buffer for cancellations and changing conversion.

Important caveat

This is a planning tool, not a guarantee. The right price for your business depends on your average job value, your actual lead-to-install conversion rate, and how much of each job you are prepared to allocate to customer acquisition. Run the numbers with your own last 60–90 days of data before committing to any provider.
