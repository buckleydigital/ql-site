The Problem

Most HVAC leads come
from the wrong places.

The typical HVAC lead in Australia comes from a directory listing, a marketplace, or a shared form that sends the same homeowner's details to every air con company in a 50km radius. The homeowner didn't choose you. They clicked a button and now four businesses are racing to call them first.

Half the time the enquiry is vague - "need air conditioning" with no detail on property size, system type, or whether they're looking for a $2,000 split system or a $15,000 ducted install. You're quoting blind against competitors who are doing the same thing, and the homeowner is picking whoever answers cheapest.

That's not a lead generation strategy. That's a race to the bottom with no control over what walks through the door.

The Economics

Why we build systems,
not sell leads.

The per-lead model in HVAC has the same structural problem it has everywhere else. The seller makes money on volume, so quality drops. You end up paying for enquiries from renters who need their landlord's permission, people shopping five quotes with no intention of moving fast, and "air con" leads that turn out to be a $90 re-gas.

We don't sell HVAC leads as a business model. We build the infrastructure that generates them and put it directly in your hands. You own the system, you own the leads, and the cost per enquiry drops the longer it runs.

A single ducted install is $8,000-$20,000+. A multi-head split system job sits at $4,000-$8,000. Even a standard single-split install is $2,000-$3,500. One ducted job from your system covers months of ad spend. Stack a few service calls on top and the pipeline pays for itself before you've finished the first install.
