Section 01

Close or Credit
Guarantee.

For any first order of 15 leads or more, QuoteLeads guarantees continued lead provision until at least one (1) deal is closed, provided the customer meets all eligibility criteria outlined in Section 4. This guarantee applies to your first order only.

This guarantee represents our commitment to delivering measurable outcomes, not merely contact information. If your first order is for 15 leads or more and you do not secure a single sale from that order, we will continue providing replacement leads at no additional charge until a deal is closed. Subsequent orders are not covered by the Close or Credit Guarantee and are subject to our standard replacement policy (Section 3).

In Plain Terms
First order of 15+ leads - Don't close a deal - We keep delivering leads until you do. No extra fees. No expiration. Just results. Applies to first order only.
Section 02

Delivery
Timeline.

Upon confirmed payment, leads from an order begin delivering within 24-48 hours. Delivery occurs over a 7-14 day period unless otherwise specified in writing at the time of order.

Lead volume is paced to ensure quality control and optimal response rates. Rush delivery may be available upon request subject to campaign capacity.

If QuoteLeads fails to deliver the full quantity of leads within the agreed delivery window, the customer is entitled to a refund for any undelivered leads, calculated at the per-lead rate paid. Refunds for undelivered leads will be processed within 5-10 business days of the customer notifying QuoteLeads in writing at contact@quoteleads.com.au.

Section 03

Verification &
Replacement Policy.

All contact information is 100% verified and all leads are in-area verified before delivery. Anything outside the initially agreed criteria is filtered out at the source.

On the rare occasion a lead slips through that does not meet your specified criteria, we replace it free of charge. Our standard replacement allowance covers up to 10% of leads under normal circumstances.

For disagreements regarding lead quality or criteria alignment, we gracefully replace up to a maximum of 20% of total order volume to maintain customer satisfaction and partnership integrity.

Section 04

Close or Credit
Eligibility Criteria.

To qualify for the Close or Credit Guarantee, the customer must demonstrate active utilisation of QuoteLeadsHQ to its full potential. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Leads must be managed inside QuoteLeadsHQ (not exported to external systems exclusively)
  • AI SMS must be active and correctly configured for immediate lead response
  • Leads must be responded to promptly (within industry-standard timeframes, ideally under 5 minutes)
  • Standard follow-up must be completed through the platform (Day 2, 5 and 10 sequences)
  • Customer must maintain a documented sales process within QuoteLeadsHQ that demonstrates genuine attempt to convert leads
Important: The Close or Credit Guarantee applies exclusively to a customer's first order of 15 leads or more. Orders below this threshold, and all subsequent orders after the first, are subject to our standard replacement policy only (Section 3) and do not qualify for indefinite lead provision.

Payment &
Refund Terms.

All payments for Pay Per Lead orders are processed at the time of order confirmation. Funds are immediately deployed to advertising platforms, campaign management, and lead generation infrastructure.

Funds cannot be recouped that have been paid directly to advertising platforms. This is non-negotiable. Once an order is placed and payment cleared, those funds are committed to active campaigns and cannot be withdrawn, refunded, or transferred.

Full stop.

Your payment activates live campaigns immediately. No refunds. No chargebacks. No exceptions. We deliver leads or credits against your order until you close a deal (if eligible) or your order volume is fulfilled.
