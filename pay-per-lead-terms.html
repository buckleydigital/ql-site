Section 01

What You Are
Buying.

A Pay Per Lead order is an order for a specified number of Leads relating to: the trade or service you select; the coverage area you select; and any other criteria shown at checkout.

The price per Lead, any applicable discount, GST and total order price will be shown at checkout before you pay.

Areas. One checkout may cover up to five suburbs, each with its own radius, a postcode list or a whole state. Each area is treated as a separate order for pricing, Lead allocation, delivery, delivery limits and assessing whether a Lead is inside the selected coverage area. The quantity selected at checkout applies separately to each area unless the checkout page clearly states otherwise. A Lead's location is assessed using the postcode or location information supplied with the enquiry.

Exclusive Leads. We will not intentionally deliver the same Lead to another business after that Lead has been delivered to you. "Exclusive" does not mean that the person has not contacted another business independently, has not seen advertising from another business, has not previously submitted an enquiry, cannot contact another business after delivery, or that you are guaranteed to be the only business the person considers.

Section 02

What Is
a Lead?

Definition. A Lead is a new enquiry submitted through a QuoteLeads website, advertising channel, form, telephone service or other lead-generation channel, where the person submitting the enquiry has requested information, contact, a quote or services relating substantially to the trade or service and area selected in your order.

A Lead may include information such as:

  • the person's name;
  • telephone number;
  • email address;
  • location;
  • property or project details; and/or
  • a description of the requested work.

The information supplied with a Lead may depend on how the enquiry was submitted. A Lead is assessed using the information reasonably available to us at or around the time of delivery.

What a Lead does not mean. A Lead is not a guarantee that the person owns or occupies the relevant property, is the final decision-maker, has a particular budget, is ready to proceed, will answer your call, will respond to your messages, will confirm that they submitted the enquiry, will agree to an appointment, will request or accept a quote, will accept your pricing, will use your services, or will proceed with the proposed work.

A person may change their mind, delay the work, select another provider or decide not to proceed. Those circumstances do not, by themselves, make a Lead invalid.

Verification. Before delivery, we may use reasonable technical and operational checks to assess the Lead, including checking whether the telephone number appears to be real, assigned or reachable and whether the location appears to be within your selected coverage area. Verification does not mean that the person will answer your call, will respond to you, that the telephone number will remain connected, that the person will confirm the enquiry, that the person has a particular level of interest, or that the person will become a customer.

Section 03

Delivery.

Delivery time. You can generally expect your first Lead within 24 to 48 hours after cleared payment. This is an estimate only. Delivery may take longer because Leads are generated in response to genuine, real-time demand in your selected area. A delay in receiving the first Lead is not, by itself, a breach of these terms. Leads will be delivered as they become available.

Unless we agree to a different schedule in writing, the full order will be delivered within 90 days after cleared payment. This period is the delivery window. The speed of delivery may depend on factors outside our control, including the number of people enquiring in your area, demand for the selected trade or service, the size of the selected coverage area, seasonal conditions, and the number of businesses advertising for similar work.

If we do not deliver the full order. If we have not delivered all ordered Leads by the end of the delivery window, you may choose to receive a refund for the undelivered Leads, or extend the delivery window. The refund will be calculated using the per-Lead price paid for the relevant order and will be paid within 10 business days after we receive your request. To request a refund or extension, contact contact@quoteleads.com.au.

If we stop an order. We may end an order by giving you written notice if we reasonably believe that we can no longer generate Leads in your selected area at the quality or volume required by your order. If we end an order, we will refund the amount paid for all undelivered Leads within 10 business days.

Section 04

Lead Quality and
Replacements.

When a Lead is valid. A Lead is valid if, when delivered:

  • it relates substantially to the trade or service selected in your order;
  • the relevant location is within your selected coverage area;
  • it contains contact information that passes our applicable verification process;
  • it is not a duplicate of a Lead previously delivered to you; and
  • it was generated through our Lead-generation process.

A Lead remains valid even if the person does not answer, does not return your call, does not respond to an SMS, email or other message, changes their mind, has no current budget, considers your price too high, delays or cancels the work, obtains other quotes, chooses another provider, or does not proceed with a quote, appointment, job, revenue or profit.

When a Lead is invalid. A Lead may be invalid if, when delivered: the telephone number was disconnected or not assigned; the telephone number belonged to a person who did not submit the enquiry; the Lead was outside your selected coverage area; the Lead did not relate substantially to the selected trade or service; the Lead was a duplicate previously delivered to you; the Lead contained materially false or fabricated information; or the Lead materially failed to meet the order criteria shown at checkout. Minor inaccuracies or later changes in the person's circumstances do not automatically make a Lead invalid.

Raising a claim. You must raise an invalid Lead claim within 7 days after delivery through the dispute function in QuoteLeadsHQ. If you cannot use QuoteLeadsHQ, you may email contact@quoteleads.com.au with the Lead reference, the date and time of delivery, the reason you believe the Lead is invalid, and any supporting information reasonably requested by us. For a claim that a telephone number is wrong, disconnected or belongs to someone who did not submit the enquiry, you must make at least one reasonable call attempt within 24 hours after delivery and record that attempt in QuoteLeadsHQ, unless this is not reasonably possible. We may request further information to assess a claim.

Replacement. If we accept that a Lead is invalid, we will replace it with a valid Lead at no additional charge. An invalid Lead is not refunded. Until it is replaced, it does not count towards your order, so if it has not been replaced by the end of the delivery window it is treated as an undelivered Lead under Section 3.

Automatic processing threshold. We may automatically process replacement claims up to 20% of the total Leads in an order. This 20% threshold concerns how claims are processed. It does not limit your right to claim a replacement for a Lead that is invalid under these terms. If claims exceed 20% of an order, we may conduct a broader review of the order. We will act reasonably and in good faith when assessing the claims.

No Guarantee of Conversion
or Business Results.

We guarantee only that a Lead will meet the requirements for a valid Lead under Section 4. We do not guarantee that any Lead will answer a telephone call, respond to a telephone call, SMS, email or other communication, confirm that they submitted the enquiry, be available for an appointment, own or occupy the relevant property, have the budget to proceed, request a quote, accept a quote, accept your pricing, engage your business, complete the proposed work, generate revenue or profit, or produce any particular conversion rate or return on investment.

We do not guarantee any particular number of answered calls, conversations, appointments, quotes, accepted quotes, jobs, customers, sales, revenue or profit.

The outcome of a Lead depends on matters outside our control, including the person's circumstances, budget and timing, your pricing, availability, qualifications and licensing, reputation, response time, communication and sales process, the quality of your follow-up, and the person's decision to use another provider or not proceed.

A valid Lead is not refundable merely because it does not convert into a conversation, appointment, quote, job, customer, revenue or profit.

Section 06

Getting the Most
From Your Leads.

Leads may be most valuable when contacted promptly. We recommend that you:

  • call or message each Lead as soon as reasonably possible;
  • respond within five minutes where practical;
  • keep any QuoteLeadsHQ automated SMS feature enabled, if available;
  • follow up appropriately if the person does not answer; and
  • record Lead outcomes in QuoteLeadsHQ.

These are recommendations only. They are not conditions of your order and do not affect your right to claim for an invalid Lead.

You must not contact a Lead in a manner that is unlawful, misleading, harassing or inconsistent with applicable privacy, telemarketing or electronic communications laws.
