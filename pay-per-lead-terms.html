Section 01

What You're
Buying.

A Pay Per Lead order is a set number of enquiries from homeowners or businesses in the trade, coverage area and lead type you choose at checkout (the "order criteria"). Each lead is exclusive: it is delivered to you and no other business.

The price per lead, any volume discount and the total including GST are shown at checkout before you pay. The minimum order is shown on the order page.

In Plain Terms
You choose the trade, area and number of leads, you see the full price before paying, and every lead goes to you alone.
Section 02

Delivery
Timeline.

Delivery starts within 24-48 hours of cleared payment. Leads arrive as they come in, and the full order is delivered within 30 days of cleared payment, or 90 days for orders of 100 leads or more (the "delivery window"), unless a different schedule is agreed in writing when you order.

If we don't deliver in time. If any leads are still undelivered when the delivery window ends, you can choose to have the remaining leads refunded at the per-lead price you paid, or to extend the window. Email contact@quoteleads.com.au and we will refund within 10 business days.

If we need to stop an order. If we can no longer generate leads in your area at the quality or volume your order needs, we may end the order by telling you in writing, and we will refund the undelivered leads at the per-lead price you paid within 10 business days.

Section 03

Lead Quality &
Replacements.

Every lead is checked before delivery: the phone number is verified as real and reachable, and the location is confirmed inside your coverage area.

If a lead you receive is not a valid lead, we will replace it at no charge. A lead is not valid if:

  • the phone number is disconnected, not in service or belongs to someone who didn't make the enquiry;
  • it is outside your coverage area;
  • it duplicates a lead we have already delivered to you; or
  • it otherwise doesn't match your order criteria.

How to raise it. Raise the lead within 7 days of delivery through the dispute button in QuoteLeadsHQ, which checks most claims automatically. If you can't use QuoteLeadsHQ, email contact@quoteleads.com.au instead. For a wrong or disconnected number, please log at least one call attempt in QuoteLeadsHQ within 24 hours of delivery, so the claim can be checked.

Volume. Replacements are handled automatically up to 20% of your order. Above that, we review the order with you and will replace or refund any further leads that are not valid. If we can't supply a replacement within the delivery window, we refund that lead instead.

A lead that meets the order criteria is a valid lead even if the person doesn't go ahead with a quote or a job.

Section 04

Getting the Most
From Your Leads.

Leads are worth most in the first few minutes. Businesses that do best with Pay Per Lead:

  • call or text each lead within 5 minutes of delivery;
  • keep the QuoteLeadsHQ AI SMS on, so every lead gets a reply in seconds, day or night;
  • follow up leads that don't answer over the following days; and
  • track each lead's outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ, so you can see what's working.

These are recommendations. They are not conditions of your order and do not affect your right to replacements, apart from the call attempt needed to check a wrong-number claim (Section 03).

Payment &
Refunds.

Payment. Orders are paid in full, including GST, at checkout. Your payment funds the advertising and campaign work for your order from the start, which is why we take payment up front.

Refunds. You are entitled to a refund for leads we don't deliver (Section 02) and for invalid leads we can't replace (Section 03). Valid leads that have been delivered aren't refundable because of a change of mind or because a lead didn't turn into a job.

No guarantee of results. We guarantee the leads, not what happens after them. Whether a lead becomes a quote, an appointment or a sale depends on many things outside our control, including how and when it is followed up.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or changes any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot be excluded.

If you're unhappy with an order, please contact us at contact@quoteleads.com.au before disputing a payment with your bank, so we can try to put it right.

You pay up front. Leads we don't deliver are refunded, invalid leads are replaced or refunded, and valid leads aren't refunded because they didn't convert. Your Australian Consumer Law rights always apply.
