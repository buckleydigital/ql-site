Section 2

The 30-Day Quoted-Jobs Guarantee.

If the System does not produce at least 10 quoted jobs during the Client's first 30 days of live campaign operation, QuoteLeads will refund the full $1,250.

The guarantee applies to the first 30 consecutive calendar days from the date the Client's ads first go live ("Guarantee Period"). It applies once only, to the Client's first System engagement, and cannot be transferred, extended, or re-applied.

The guarantee is measured on quoted jobs, and applies only where the Client has met the qualifying conditions in Section 4, including working the leads the System delivers. It does not extend to sales, revenue, or profit, which depend on the Client's own pricing and sales process and are expressly excluded.

Section 3

What counts as a quoted job.

A "quoted job" is a platform-delivered lead the Client has given a genuine price quote to, by phone or on-site, for a lead who engaged (answered and discussed their job), with that quote recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ during the Guarantee Period. A lead that could not be reached, or that did not genuinely engage, is not a quoted job, for either party's purposes.

Whether the count of 10 has been reached is assessed from QuoteLeadsHQ records: the leads delivered, worked, and marked quoted. If the records show fewer than 10 quoted jobs across the Guarantee Period and the conditions in Section 4 were met, the refund is owed.

Section 4

The conditions to qualify.

Because a quote requires the Client to actually contact and quote the leads, the guarantee assumes the Client gave the System a fair chance to work. To qualify, all of the following must be true, and these are the only conditions, each verifiable from QuoteLeadsHQ or ad-account data:

  • Ads were funded and ran at the agreed budget for the Guarantee Period, from the Client's own ad account.
  • The AI SMS follow-up was switched on and active in QuoteLeadsHQ for the Guarantee Period. This is what puts leads in front of the Client ready to quote.
  • The Client worked the leads delivered, contacting and quoting the genuine, contactable homeowners the System sent, and recording the outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ.

Where the Client switched the follow-up off, ran ads below the agreed budget for a material part of the period, or left delivered leads unworked, the guarantee does not apply, because in that case the System was not given the chance to reach the target. Brief, unavoidable interruptions do not void the guarantee.

There are no other conditions. No minimum personal response time, no required daily activity, no paperwork gauntlet.

Section 5

What is refunded.

Where a valid claim is made, QuoteLeads refunds the full $1,250 paid by the Client for the System, in full. This is the sole remedy under this guarantee and it is a genuine monetary refund.

Advertising spend is not refundable. Advertising budget is paid by the Client directly to Meta (or other third-party advertising platforms) from the Client's own advertising account. QuoteLeads never receives or holds that money and therefore cannot refund it. This is a factual limitation, not an exclusion. The Client keeps full control and visibility of their own ad spend throughout.

Section 6

How to claim.

  • Submit a claim in writing to contact@quoteleads.com.au within 14 days after the Guarantee Period ends.
  • QuoteLeads verifies from QuoteLeadsHQ and ad-account data: that ads ran funded, follow-up was active, the Client worked the delivered leads (Section 4), and that fewer than 10 jobs were quoted (Section 3).
  • Where these are confirmed, the $1,250 refund is issued within 14 days of the claim.

Because qualification is verified from platform data, there is no dispute over "effort" and no discretionary judgement by QuoteLeads.

First engagement only. This guarantee is available on the Client's first System engagement and is not available to returning, reactivated, or restructured accounts.

No other outcome representations. Other than the quoted-jobs guarantee set out above, QuoteLeads makes no representation as to leads, quotes, sales, revenue, or profit. Platform performance is also influenced by factors outside QuoteLeads' control, including the Client's pricing, responsiveness, sales process, market conditions, and local competition.
