Meta Business Access Guide

How to grant partner access to your Meta assets - takes less than 5 minutes.

⚡

Why we set it up this way

By connecting your Page and Ad Account under your own Meta Business portfolio, you own every asset from day one. This avoids complicated transfer processes if you ever change agencies.

1
Open Meta Business Settings

Go to business.facebook.com/settings and log in with your Facebook account.

If you see "Select business" with no options, click Create Business to set up your Business Portfolio first.

2
Navigate to Partners

In the left sidebar click Settings (gear icon), then under Users select Partners.

3
Add QuoteLeads as a Partner

Click the Add button (top right). Select "Give a partner access to your assets".

4
Enter QuoteLeads' Business ID

Paste the Business ID into the dialog field, then click Next.

QuoteLeads Business ID
1065750507426304
5
Assign Your Facebook Page

Select your Page from the dropdown, then set permissions as shown below.

Permission Status Description
Content ON Create, manage or delete posts, stories and more as the Page
Community activity ON Review and respond to comments, remove unwanted content
Messages and calls OFF Send and respond to messages and calls as the Page
Ads ON Create, manage and delete ads for the Page
Insights ON See how the Page, content and ads perform
Revenue ON View and export Page monetisation and earnings data
Leads ON Access and manage leads, including downloading leads
6
Assign Your Ad Account

Select your Ad Account from the dropdown. Enable Full control - Manage ad accounts.

Permission Status Description
Manage campaigns (ads) OFF Create and edit ads, access reports and view ads
View performance OFF Access reports and view ads
Manage Creative Hub mockups OFF View, create and edit mockups in Creative Hub
Manage ad accounts ON ✓ Full control - Control ad account settings, finances and permissions
7
Confirm - You're Done!

Click Save. You'll see the "Assets assigned" confirmation screen - that's it, you're all set!
