30-Day ROI Guarantee.

QuoteLeads provides the following limited guarantee to Clients who have operated the platform in strict compliance with all required protocols during their first engagement. This guarantee applies once only, to the initial 30-day campaign window, and cannot be transferred, extended, or re-applied under any circumstances.

The guarantee: If, after the first 30 consecutive calendar days of active ad campaign operation, the Client can demonstrate - with documented evidence - that no revenue was generated from platform-delivered leads sufficient to cover the combined platform management fee and ad spend for that period, QuoteLeads will provide one additional 30-day platform management period at no further platform management fee.

All of the following conditions must be strictly and fully satisfied to qualify. Failure to satisfy any single condition voids the guarantee in its entirety:

  • The 30-day period must be the first 30 consecutive calendar days from the date the Client's ads first went live - no later window qualifies
  • Advertising campaigns must have run without a single day of interruption or pause during the full 30-day period - any pause of any duration, for any reason, immediately voids the guarantee
  • The Client must not have requested, suggested, or caused any modification to campaign settings, budgets, creative, copy, targeting, or any other campaign variable during the 30-day period
  • The ad spend budget must have been maintained at or above the originally agreed level for every day of the 30-day period without reduction
  • AI SMS must have been continuously active and without interruption for the full 30-day period, with no gap in SMS credit balance at any point
  • The Client must have personally contacted 100% of all delivered leads within 5 minutes of receipt - any lead not contacted within this window disqualifies the entire claim
  • Structured follow-ups must have been completed on Days 2, 5, and 10 for every single delivered lead
  • All lead outcomes must have been recorded in the QuoteLeadsHQ CRM within 48 hours of each interaction
  • All creative, copy, and access approvals during the platform build phase must have been returned to QuoteLeads within 24 hours of each request
  • All platform fees and ad spend invoices must be paid in full with zero outstanding balance at the time the claim is submitted
  • No disputes, payment challenges, or billing inquiries may have been raised by the Client at any point prior to or during the 30-day period
  • The Client must not be in breach of any provision of these Terms, the QuoteLeads Terms & Conditions, or any platform usage policy at the time of the claim
  • The guarantee claim must be submitted in writing to contact@quoteleads.com.au no later than 7 days after the 30-day period concludes - claims submitted after this window will not be considered under any circumstances
  • The claim must be accompanied by: timestamped CRM records for every lead interaction, documented evidence of all follow-up activity, and a written statement of all revenue outcomes from delivered leads during the period
  • QuoteLeads retains sole and absolute discretion to assess whether the qualifying conditions have been met. QuoteLeads' determination is final and binding

This guarantee does not cover and cannot be applied to:

  • Advertising spend paid directly to third-party platforms - non-refundable in all circumstances
  • The platform build fee, governed separately under Section 5
  • Revenue, profit, or sales conversion outcomes, which remain subject to Section 2
  • Any period affected by Client interference, as defined under Section 7
  • Underperformance caused or contributed to by the Client's own pricing, quoting speed, availability, sales skills, or responsiveness
  • Engagements beyond the Client's first - this guarantee is not available to returning, reactivated, or restructured Client accounts

Remedy: Where QuoteLeads determines, in its sole discretion, that all conditions have been met and a valid claim has been properly submitted, QuoteLeads will provide one additional 30-day platform management period at no further platform management fee. This is the sole and exclusive remedy under this guarantee. No monetary refund, credit, or compensation of any kind is available under this guarantee.

Section 4

Platform Build & Delivery.

QuoteLeads will build and configure the Client's platform system following receipt of all required account access and credentials. The build window is up to 7 business days from that date.

The Client is required to provide all necessary account access, credentials, approvals, and creative assets within 7 calendar days of receiving the QuoteLeads onboarding request. Where the Client fails to provide any required item within this window, the 30-day platform management period and associated billing will commence on day 7 from the date of the onboarding request regardless of whether all assets have been received. QuoteLeads will proceed with available assets and complete the build as materials are provided, however the management period will not be delayed or paused due to Client inaction.

In practice, QuoteLeads aims to have initial advertising previews delivered to the Client within 24-48 hours of receiving all required access, with systems fully configured and ready to go live within 3-5 business days.

Definition of delivery: Platform delivery is confirmed at the point QuoteLeads sends the Client their initial advertising previews. This constitutes delivery of the built system - the platform is configured, the CRM is ready to receive leads, and the advertising infrastructure is complete and ready to activate.

Once initial advertising previews are provided to the Client, the platform build is considered complete and the build fee is fully earned. Refund eligibility is governed exclusively by Section 5.

Following delivery, the Client's platform enters a 30-day management and optimisation window. During this period, QuoteLeads monitors platform performance, refines campaign delivery, and makes optimisation adjustments at no additional cost. This period begins on the date the Client's ads first go live. Any pausing of ads during this period is the Client's responsibility and does not extend or reset the window.

Section 5

Build Fee - Non-Refundable Upon Delivery.

The build fee is non-refundable in all circumstances once initial advertising previews have been provided by QuoteLeads. This applies regardless of the Client's decision to activate the system, changes in business circumstances, dissatisfaction with creative output, or any other reason.

The sole exception is where QuoteLeads has failed to deliver initial advertising previews within 7 business days of receiving all required account access from the Client, and that failure is not caused or contributed to by the Client (including any delay in providing required credentials, approvals, or information). In that event only, a refund of the build fee may be requested in writing.

A refund will only be considered where all of the following conditions are met:
