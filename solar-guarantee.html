Questions

Answered straight.

What if I don't get 90?

We keep running and improving the system with no management fee until you have your 90. Your ad spend still goes straight to Meta. If 90 fee-free days pass without reaching it, either of us can walk away with nothing further owed.

Does it count if I don't win the job?

Yes. We control who comes through the funnel, what they tell us and whether they confirm. We can't control the quote or the sale, so the guarantee counts confirmations. Log every call in QuoteLeadsHQ within 24 hours, or that lead counts toward your 90.

Why does the ad spend go up after 30 days?

The first month finds what works at $50 a day. From day 31 we put more behind it, at $100 a day, which is what gets you to 90.

What happens if I leave?

You keep the ad account, the funnel and its domain, the survey data and every lead. It was all built in your name. Leaving ends the guarantee.

Who is this not for?

Anyone who can't fund the ad spend, anyone who won't call leads back within a day, and anyone whose service area is too small to find 90 homeowners in 90 days. We'll tell you on the call if your area is too tight.
