The Problem

Most solar leads are built
to sell, not to convert.

The standard model in solar lead generation is volume over quality. A homeowner fills out a generic form, their details get sold to three, four, sometimes five solar companies. Everyone races to dial first. The homeowner gets bombarded by strangers they never agreed to hear from, and by the time you call, they've already spoken to two competitors and stopped answering the phone.

The economics of that model only work for the company selling the leads. For the solar business buying them, it's a numbers game that gets more expensive every quarter - and every competitor in your area is playing the same game with the same leads from the same sources.

The Economics

Why we build systems,
not sell leads.

The per-lead model in solar creates a structural problem. When the seller's revenue depends on moving volume, the incentive is to generate as many leads as possible from the cheapest sources - recycled data, low-intent forms, shared enquiries. Quality suffers because quality is expensive.

We don't sell solar leads as an ongoing business model. Marking up every enquiry and clipping the ticket doesn't work long term for either side. Instead, we build the lead generation infrastructure directly into your business. You own the system, you own the leads, and the cost per enquiry drops the longer it runs.

The average residential solar installation in Australia is $7,000–$12,000+. One closed job from your system covers weeks of ad spend. The maths only gets better the longer the system runs.
