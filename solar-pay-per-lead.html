FAQ

Common Questions

What types of solar leads do you offer? +

We offer two main lead types: Solar Installation leads and Battery Storage leads. Each can be filtered for Residential or Commercial properties. You choose which types match your business focus.

How are leads qualified? +

Every lead answers custom qualification questions including property details, energy usage, budget range, timeline, and decision-making authority. We verify contact details before delivery to ensure you're only getting serious prospects.

How do I receive the leads? +

Leads can be delivered directly to your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, etc.), via SMS and email alerts, or through our optional platform dashboard. You choose the method that works best for your workflow.

Are the leads really exclusive? +

Yes. Every lead is 100% exclusive to your business in your service area. We never share leads with multiple companies. When you receive a lead, you're the only solar installer they're talking to.

What areas do you cover? +

We generate leads across Australia. You specify your service territory by postcode or region, and we only deliver leads from those areas. No wasted leads from outside your coverage zone.

Is there a lock-in contract? +

No. There's no lock-in contract and no minimum commitment. You can pause, adjust, or cancel your lead delivery at any time. You're in complete control.
