The offer
Quote-ready solar enquiries for less than $100 in ad spend each, averaged across your first 30 days, or we waive our management fee until we hit it.

This is a cost-per-enquiry guarantee, not a sales, installs or revenue guarantee. If the average is $100 or more, we keep managing and running your system with no management fee until a 30-day period averages less than $100, for up to 3 months. The Client always pays its own ad spend, direct to Meta. Available to eligible Australian solar businesses that satisfy every condition below.

Overview

What this Promotion is.

This Promotion is a performance-based offer made available by QuoteLeads (ABN 83 894 208 171) ("QuoteLeads") to eligible Clients under Section 17 (Promotions and Special Offers) of the QuoteLeads Branded Lead Gen System Terms (the "Principal Terms").

The offer: across the Measurement Period, the Client's average Cost per Quote-Ready Enquiry will be less than $100. If it is not, QuoteLeads will continue to manage and run the Client's system with no management fee until a later 30-day period averages less than $100, provided the Client has met every condition in these Promotional Terms.

Ad spend, not our fee. The $100 figure is advertising spend billed by Meta to the Client's own ad account, divided by Quote-Ready Enquiries. It is not a price QuoteLeads charges per lead. QuoteLeads' fees are the build fee and the monthly management fee set out in the Principal Terms.

Not a sales guarantee. QuoteLeads does not guarantee, and these Promotional Terms do not create any guarantee of, quotes issued, installs, conversion rates, signed contracts, revenue, cash flow, profit or margin. Any example of cost per install shown in QuoteLeads' marketing is an illustration only and depends on the Client's own close rate.

Solar campaigns only. This Promotion applies to solar, solar + battery and battery retrofit campaigns. It does not apply to other trades.

Australia only. This Promotion is available solely to businesses located in Australia. These Promotional Terms form part of the agreement between the parties in respect of this Promotion, in accordance with Section 17 of the Principal Terms. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Principal Terms.

Definitions

What the words mean.

"Quote-Ready Enquiry" means an enquiry delivered through the managed system and recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ from a person who:

  • (a) completed the full survey on the Client's funnel, answering every question;
  • (b) is located inside the Client's service area as agreed in writing at onboarding;
  • (c) stated that they own the home the enquiry is about;
  • (d) has a verified, working phone number; and
  • (e) responded, either by replying to the AI SMS agent or by answering a call from the Client, or is counted under Condition 5 because the Client did not log three call attempts within 24 hours.

It excludes spam, test, duplicate, existing-customer, supplier, job-seeker and sales enquiries, and enquiries generated outside the managed system.

"Measurement Period" means the 30 consecutive days starting on the day the Client's managed ads first go live.

"Cost per Quote-Ready Enquiry" means the total advertising spend billed by Meta for the managed campaigns during a 30-day period, excluding GST, divided by the number of Quote-Ready Enquiries recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ for the same period.

Eligibility

Who can apply.

To be eligible, the Client must at the time of acceptance and throughout the Measurement Period:

  • Be an Australian solar business selling and installing solar, solar + battery or battery systems.
  • Be on a managed QuoteLeads build (Standard or Growth) with $100 per day or more in ad spend.
  • Have the capacity to call, quote and service the enquiries within its normal lead times.
  • Be able to meet all seven conditions set out below.

QuoteLeads may request reasonable evidence of capacity or budget before accepting a Client into the Promotion, and may decline or withdraw the Promotion where it reasonably believes the Client does not meet these requirements or has obtained or used it dishonestly or in breach of the Principal Terms, consistent with Section 17.

Conditions to apply

You must meet all seven conditions.

The Client must satisfy all seven of the following conditions throughout the Measurement Period, and throughout any later period in which the management fee is waived. Failure to meet any condition voids the guarantee and releases QuoteLeads from the fee-waiver obligation.

  • 1
    Fund at least $100 per day in ad spend The Client must fund advertising spend of at least $100 per day (around $3,000 per 30 days) on the managed campaigns, paid directly to Meta from the Client's own ad account, for the whole Measurement Period. Reducing spend below $100 per day, or failing to fund it, voids the guarantee.
  • 2
    Keep the ads running for 30 consecutive days The managed campaigns must run for the full Measurement Period without being paused, switched off or interrupted by the Client.
  • 3
    Approve creative change requests within 24 hours When QuoteLeads asks for approval of new or changed ad creative, copy or targeting, the Client must approve it or respond with workable changes within 24 hours. The Client must also respond to other campaign communications from QuoteLeads within 24 hours.
  • 4
    Keep the AI SMS agent switched on The QuoteLeads AI SMS agent must stay active on every lead throughout the Measurement Period. Disabling it, pausing it or replacing it with another follow-up system voids the guarantee.
  • 5
    Work every lead and log it in QuoteLeadsHQ The Client must attempt to call every lead and log each call attempt and its outcome in QuoteLeadsHQ. Where a lead has not had at least three logged call attempts within 24 hours of arriving, that lead is counted as a Quote-Ready Enquiry for the purposes of this guarantee.
  • 6
    Keep the agreed service area, offer and creative The service area (postcodes or radius) and the offer are agreed in writing at onboarding. The Client must not reduce the service area, change the offer, or require QuoteLeads to run creative or an offer it did not build or approve, during the Measurement Period.
  • 7
    Track everything in QuoteLeadsHQ from Day 1 All leads, call attempts, quotes and outcomes must be recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ from the first day of the Measurement Period. No off-platform tracking.
Mechanics

How the guarantee works.

Day 30 scorecard. At the end of the Measurement Period, QuoteLeads calculates the Cost per Quote-Ready Enquiry from the ad spend billed by Meta and the Quote-Ready Enquiries recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ, and shares the figures with the Client.

If it is less than $100. The guarantee is satisfied, regardless of how many of those enquiries the Client quotes, wins or installs.

If it is $100 or more. Provided every condition has been met, the Client's monthly management fee is waived and QuoteLeads keeps managing and optimising the campaigns. The figure is recalculated over each following 30-day period, and the fee stays waived until a 30-day period averages less than $100. The normal monthly management fee resumes from the start of the next billing period after that.

Three-month cap. The management fee is waived for a maximum of three consecutive 30-day periods after the Measurement Period. If a 30-day period has still not averaged less than $100 by the end of that third period, the fee waiver ends and either party may end the management engagement with no further management fees payable. The normal monthly management fee applies to any management the Client chooses to continue after that point.

Leaving at any time. The Client may stop management at any time, without notice. Doing so ends this guarantee and any fee waiver from that point. The campaigns, ad account, funnel and lead data stay with the Client, as set out in the Principal Terms.

Important: The fee waiver is the only remedy under this guarantee. It is not a refund of the build fee or of any ad spend. The Client continues to fund at least $100 per day in ad spend, paid directly to Meta, throughout any waived period.

Measurement and disputes. QuoteLeads determines whether an enquiry is a Quote-Ready Enquiry acting reasonably, based on the records in QuoteLeadsHQ and the funnel. Any dispute about counting or measurement is dealt with under Section 16 (Dispute Resolution) of the Principal Terms.

Any such conduct voids the Promotion and releases QuoteLeads from all obligations under it.
