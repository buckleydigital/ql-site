90-day guarantee
promotional terms.

Confirmed quote requests in 90 days, or we work for free. These terms apply to the trade named in your agreement. Your guaranteed number (your "Number") is set out in the Schedule for that trade. Your survey questions are agreed with you at onboarding. Read with our Branded Lead Gen System Terms.

Promotional Terms under Section 17 Version 26 November 2026
Section 1

The offer.

  • This is a limited promotional offer from QuoteLeads for Australian businesses with an active ABN in a trade listed in the Schedule. One per business. We may withdraw or change it for new sign-ups at any time. Anyone who has already signed up keeps the terms they signed up on.
  • The build fee is the amount quoted to you, plus GST, paid before the build starts. It includes the first 30 days of management from the day your ads go live.
  • After that, ongoing management is the monthly fee quoted to you, plus GST, month to month.
  • Ad spend is not included. It is billed by Meta directly to your own ad account.
  • We register a new domain for your funnel in your name, separate from your existing website, and hand the funnel over to you in full. The first year's domain registration is included. Renewals after that are yours.
Section 2

The 48-hour launch.

  • Within 48 hours (business days, AEST) of access being granted to your Meta Page, ad account and Pixel, your ads, funnel and survey are built and ready for your approval to go live.
Section 3

The guarantee.

  • We guarantee your Number of confirmed quote requests within the guarantee period. The guarantee period is the first 90 counted days from the day your ads first go live (see condition 1 for what makes a day count).
  • If the guarantee period ends with fewer than your Number, we keep managing the system with no management fee from then until your Number is reached (the fee-free period). Management fees start again from the day after that.
  • The guarantee needs our management in place for the whole guarantee period. The first 30 days are included in the build fee. Days 31 to 90 are at the monthly management fee in section 1.
  • The guarantee is the free management described above. It is not a refund of the build fee or of any management fee already paid, and it does not cover ad spend.
  • The guarantee applies once, to your first 90 days. It doesn't repeat for later periods.
  • If a fee-free period reaches 90 days without your Number being reached, either of us can end the arrangement in writing. Nothing further is owed for management, and section 7 applies to what you keep.
  • Any dollar figure we give for the value of your quote requests is an illustration based on an average job price. It is not part of the guarantee. The guarantee is your Number of confirmed quote requests.
Section 4

What counts as a confirmed quote request.

A confirmed quote request is a quote-ready lead who then confirms. A lead confirms in one of the two ways below, whichever happens first.

Quote-ready lead

  • A homeowner, or the decision-maker for the property, in the service area agreed at onboarding, who submitted through your funnel, gave their name, phone number, email and postcode, and answered every qualifying question shown to them.
  • The qualifying questions are the ones agreed with you at onboarding for your business.
  • A lead who says they rent the home doesn't count.
  • Answers are the lead's own. We don't check them against documents. Any upload (for example a power bill or photos) is an optional extra step for the lead. We don't guarantee that any lead will upload anything, and a lead counts whether or not they do.
  • Your questions are tailored to your business. The guarantee applies to funnels with up to 8 qualifying questions, not counting contact details and location. The agreed questions are recorded in writing at onboarding. Changing them after launch needs both of us to agree, and the guarantee period restarts from the change. While a funnel asks more than 8, the guarantee doesn't apply.

Way 1: confirmed by text

  • The lead replies by SMS to the AI SMS follow-up sent in your business's name, and the reply says they want a quote, a call about a quote, or to go ahead with their enquiry. Examples: "Yes", "Yes please", a preferred call time, or details of the job they want.
  • These replies don't confirm a lead: a refusal ("No", "Not anymore"), a wrong number, a request to stop messages, a reply saying they've chosen another provider, a reply that isn't from a homeowner (for example another business), spam, or no reply at all.

Way 2: not called within 24 hours

  • You are expected to call every lead within 24 hours of it being delivered to QuoteLeadsHQ, and to log the call there within the same 24 hours. To log a call, either add a note to the lead (for example "Called 2:15pm, no answer") or move the lead out of New lead to the stage that fits (for example No answer or Follow up).
  • A lead with no call logged within 24 hours counts as a confirmed quote request.
  • Saturdays and Sundays don't count toward the 24 hours. A lead delivered on a Friday is due by the same time on Monday. A lead delivered on a weekend is due by the end of Monday.

Counting

  • A lead counts once, on the day it first confirms.
  • A lead with the same phone number or email as one counted in the previous 90 days is a duplicate and doesn't count again.
  • Whether you quote or win the job doesn't affect whether a confirmed quote request counts.
  • Leads who don't confirm are still delivered to you. They just don't count toward your Number.
Section 5

Checking the count.

  • Every lead, every SMS reply, every note and every stage change is recorded in QuoteLeadsHQ with its date and time, where you can see them at any time. That record is what we both rely on.
  • We work out the count from that record at day 30, day 60 and the end of the guarantee period, and at any time you ask for it. We'll send you the list of leads that counted and why.
  • If you think a lead was counted that shouldn't have been, or missed when it should have counted, tell us at contact@quoteleads.com.au within 7 days of us sending you the count. We'll review the conversation with you and correct the count if it's wrong.
  • A lead isn't removed from the count because you couldn't reach them later, or because they didn't go ahead.
Section 6

Your three conditions.

Condition 1: keep your ads on

  • Your ads must run on your own Meta ad account at a daily budget of at least $50 for the first 30 counted days, and at least $100 for every counted day after that, through the guarantee period and any fee-free period. Your ad account and its payment method must stay active.
  • A day counts only if your ads were live and the budget was at least the minimum for that whole day. A day that falls short for a reason on your side (a paused campaign, a lowered budget, a declined card, an account restriction caused by your business or content, or waiting on your approval) doesn't count, and is added to the end of the guarantee period or fee-free period.
  • The guarantee period isn't complete, and the guarantee can't be triggered, until 90 counted days have run.

Condition 2: keep the AI SMS follow-up on, 24/7

  • The AI SMS follow-up in QuoteLeadsHQ must stay switched on for your account and for every lead, at all hours, for the whole guarantee period and any fee-free period. That means the AI agent is active, the first text to each new lead is on, and automatic replies are on. We switch all three on when your account is set up.
  • It's how a lead confirms. If any of the three is switched off for your account or for individual leads, set to out-of-hours only, or the messages are edited so they no longer ask the lead to confirm, the guarantee no longer applies.
  • Your account must keep enough SMS credit for the follow-up to send. Days without credit are treated as days the follow-up was off.

Condition 3: call every lead within 24 hours

  • Call each lead within 24 hours of it being delivered to QuoteLeadsHQ, and log the call there within the same 24 hours, as set out in section 4. Saturdays and Sundays don't count toward the 24 hours.
  • A lead with no call logged in time counts as a confirmed quote request. Unlike conditions 1 and 2, missing this one doesn't stop the guarantee applying. It only changes how that lead is counted.

Other conditions

  • The guarantee applies while the service area, offer and targeting stay as agreed at onboarding unless we agree a change, and you give us the access, approvals and information we need, when we ask.
  • Management fees for any paid month must be up to date for the guarantee to apply.
  • We may decline the offer at onboarding if your service area or budget makes your Number in 90 days unrealistic. If we do, we refund anything you've paid.
Section 7

Leaving.

  • There is no lock-in. You can end management at any time by emailing contact@quoteleads.com.au. Ending it ends the guarantee, including any fee-free period.
  • The ad account, Page, Pixel, funnel, funnel domain, creative, survey data and every lead stay yours either way. QuoteLeadsHQ is our platform, so the AI SMS follow-up and CRM pipeline stop when your access ends. You can export your leads before that.
Section 8

General.

  • These are Promotional Terms under Section 17 of our Branded Lead Gen System Terms. Those terms apply to your system. Where they differ from these terms, these terms apply to this offer.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law.
  • Lead and confirmation numbers depend on your market, offer and budget. The guarantee is what we commit to if the numbers fall short. It isn't a prediction of results.
