FAQ

Common Questions

What exactly do you mean by "guaranteed" appointments and sales? +

We commit in writing to minimum performance benchmarks: 80%+ contact rate, 60%+ lead-to-appointment conversion, and 25%+ appointment-to-sale conversion for engaged clients. These are documented minimums based on our track record. They are satisfaction-based guarantees—not monetary guarantees—meaning if we fall short, we work with you at no extra cost to resolve it. They do not constitute financial advice or a promise of specific dollar returns.

How does the 30-day satisfaction guarantee work? +

Within your first 30 days, if you're not satisfied with lead quality, appointment rates, or the platform, contact us and we'll work to resolve the issue—whether that's campaign adjustments, additional onboarding support, or platform configuration. The guarantee is a commitment to your satisfaction with the service, not a cash refund. Full terms are provided at sign-up.

How quickly will I receive my first solar leads? +

Campaigns go live within 24-48 hours of your strategy call and initial onboarding. Most clients receive their first qualified lead within the first 48 hours of going live.

Are these leads shared with other solar companies? +

Never. Every lead generated through your campaign is exclusive to your business in your service area. We don't resell, share, or duplicate leads. One lead, one company.

Do I need an existing CRM or can I use your platform? +

You can use QuoteLeads as your complete system—it includes lead intake, AI follow-up, appointment booking, pipeline tracking, and auto quote drafting. If you have an existing CRM, we can push leads to it automatically.

Is there a lock-in contract? +

No. Our full service is month-to-month. If you're buying leads individually, there's no commitment at all—purchase when you need them. You can pause or cancel at any time.
