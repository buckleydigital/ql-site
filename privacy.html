Overview

The short version.

QuoteLeads ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. Here's what matters most before the full detail.

What we collect
Contact & usage data
Business details, lead data, and how you use the platform. No sensitive financial or health data.
Why we collect it
To run the system
Generating leads, managing pipelines, providing support, and improving the platform.
Who we share with
Service providers only
Hosting, CRM, AI tools, and analytics partners. Never sold to third parties.
Security standard
256-bit encryption
Questions? Contact us at contact@quoteleads.com.au - we respond to general inquiries within 5 business days.
Section 01

What information
we collect.

Information you provide directly

Account information includes your full name, business name and ABN/ACN (if applicable), email, phone number, and job title. Lead information - when you use the platform - includes customer contact details, project details, communication history, and notes. Support communications include messages you send us, survey responses, feedback, and phone call recordings (with notice).

Information we collect automatically

Usage data such as pages visited, features used, time on platform, click patterns, device type, OS, browser, IP address, and referral source. Also technical data including log files, error reports, performance metrics, and cookie data (see Section 8).

Information from third parties

When you connect third-party services we receive integration data: contact and deal information from CRMs, appointment data from calendar services, message history from communication tools, and transaction confirmations from payment processors.

Note: We do not collect sensitive personal information such as health data, financial account numbers, biometric data, or government identifiers - except ABN/ACN for business verification.

Section 03

Our legal basis
for processing.

We process your information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) based on the following grounds.

  • Contract performance - Necessary to provide the platform and services you've engaged
  • Legitimate business interests - Operating our platform, fraud prevention, and service improvement
  • Legal compliance - Meeting Australian Privacy Principles and regulatory obligations
  • Consent - Where you've given permission; you can withdraw consent at any time

Section 08

Cookies and
tracking.

Cookies are small text files that help our platform function and let us analyse usage. We use them across three categories.

Essential cookies (always on)
  • Authentication and login state
  • Security features and load balancing
  • Session management
Optional cookies
  • Analytics - Google Analytics for usage patterns; conversion tracking for ad performance
  • Advertising - Facebook Pixel for retargeting; Google Ads for campaign tracking
Your choices

On your first visit, a consent banner lets you accept, reject, or customise optional cookies. You can also opt out directly via Google Analytics, Google Ads settings, or Facebook Ads settings.

Section 10

Your privacy rights.

Regardless of location, you have the following rights over your personal information.

  • Access - Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction - Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion - Request deletion, subject to legal requirements
  • Portability - Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection - Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction - Request we limit processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent - If processing is consent-based, withdraw it at any time
How to exercise your rights

Update information directly in your account settings. For all other requests, email contact@quoteleads.com.au with your name, account email, and the specific right you're exercising. We'll verify your identity, respond within 30 days (extendable to 60 days for complex requests), and won't charge a fee for most requests.

Section 12

Children's privacy.

QuoteLeads is a B2B platform designed for professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we discover we have, we'll delete it immediately. Contact contact@quoteleads.com.au if you have concerns.

Section 13

Data controller vs.
data processor.

When we are the data controller

For your business and account information - company details, contact info, billing data, platform usage, and communications with us - we decide how and why your data is processed.

When we are the data processor

For your customer (lead) data - their contact details, project information, and communications - you are the data controller. We process it only on your instructions.

Your responsibilities as data controller
  • Obtain proper consent from your customers
  • Provide your customers with a privacy notice
  • Ensure you have lawful basis to process their data
  • Honour your customers' privacy rights requests
We provide a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) outlining our data handling practices. Request one at contact@quoteleads.com.au.
Section 11

Australian Privacy Act
compliance.

We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). As an Australian platform operating exclusively in Australia, all your data is protected under Australian privacy law.

Your Australian Privacy Rights
  • Right to access - Request a copy of your personal information held by us
  • Right to correction - Request we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to complain - Lodge a complaint about how we handle your personal information
  • Right to request deletion - Request deletion of your information where appropriate

To exercise these rights or lodge a complaint, contact contact@quoteleads.com.au. We'll investigate and respond within 30 days.

Section 14

Data breach
notification.

If a breach occurs affecting your personal information, we will investigate and contain the issue immediately, notify you within 72 hours of discovery where required by law, notify relevant supervisory authorities, and provide clear information about what happened, what data was affected, what we're doing about it, and what you should do.

Report security concerns to contact@quoteleads.com.au - urgent issues receive a response within 24 hours.

Sections 15 & 16

Do Not Track &
SMS messaging.

Do Not Track signals

We don't currently respond to Do Not Track signals as there is no universal standard. Use the opt-out tools in Section 8 to control tracking. If DNT standards are established we'll update this policy accordingly.

SMS & text messaging

We comply with the Do Not Call Register Act 2006 (Cth). By providing your mobile number you give 1:1 named consent to receive service-related messages from QuoteLeads. Your details are collected and handled in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). To opt out at any time email contact@quoteleads.com.au or update your preferences in account settings. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing.
