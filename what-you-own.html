The handover

A checklist,
not a claim.

Every line below is something a lawyer would recognise. Ad accounts in your name. Domain in your name. Files on your drive. Data exportable any time. Read it like a contract.

What you keep
Meta & Google Ads accounts
Both accounts created under your business name and ABN. Campaigns, audiences and creative history live inside them.
Admin access transferred to you on day one. You can revoke ours any time.
Domain
Registered in your business name at the registrar. Not a sub-domain. Not pointing at our infrastructure by force.
Registrar login handed to you. Move it, keep it, point it anywhere.
Landing page files
The funnel built for your trade and territory - every HTML file, image and survey step that converts traffic into leads.
Delivered in a hosting account. Hosted forever for $0 per month - no hidden fees.
All lead data
Every submission, contact detail, survey response and conversation. No locked formats, no retention games.
Full CSV export on request, any time. Keep a copy, move to any CRM.
What the system runs on (not owned)
CRM pipeline
Your leads flow through a pipeline on the QuoteLeads platform, configured around how you actually close jobs.
Runs on our platform. The data inside it is yours - export any time.
AI SMS & follow-up Optional
Replies in your business name within 60 seconds, qualifies, and follows up on day 2, 5 and 10 until the job closes.
Runs on our platform. Pause or switch off any time.
