1:1 consent enquiries only - and you get the proof

Before a homeowner submits, they see and accept specific consent naming your business. This is real consent shown at form level, not a vague marketplace opt-in buried behind lead resale.

Landing page consent Required

I agree to be contacted by Your Business regarding my enquiry. I understand this is obligation-free and my details will be shared only with them. I have read and accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

This exact text is what the homeowner reads and accepts before their details are sent. Your Business is replaced with your trading name on the live form.

Delivered with every lead

The exact consent wording plus the timestamp of when the homeowner ticked it is delivered with the lead details. Your team knows exactly what was agreed, when it was agreed, and that consent was directed specifically to your business.

Compliance protection for your business

Under the Australian Privacy Act and Spam Act, your business is responsible for outreach. Most marketplaces use broad consent like "solar companies" and resell details to multiple businesses, leaving callers unable to prove what was agreed to or when. QuoteLeads sends named-business consent evidence with every lead to help protect you in disputes, complaints, and regulatory exposure. Most Australian competitors cannot replicate this because their lead sources do not capture consent this way.
