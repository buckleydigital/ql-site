Questions

Questions. Answered straight.

Because of the filtering and verification we apply before a lead is ever delivered. Every lead is exclusive, fully contactable, and already matched to your service area. The goal is lower cost per closed job, not the lowest possible cost per lead.
Every number is checked against Australian phone carriers, and a disconnected number is replaced free of charge. Every lead's postcode is also checked against your service area before it's sent to you. A lead is only replaced for location if the postcode it came with isn't in your agreed postcode list. See the Pay Per Lead Terms for the full rules.
Before any prospect submits their enquiry, they see a consent form that shows your business name explicitly. They agree to be contacted by your business specifically, not by a generic lead pool, not by five competitors. By the time a lead lands in your inbox, they already know who you are and they're expecting your call. That level of consent is rare in this industry. We think it should be standard.
More than most. For solar, every lead completes a minimum survey covering their name, email, phone and postcode, plus their average quarterly electricity bill and their purchase timeline. These two extra data points alone tell you whether someone is motivated and ready to move, not just curious. Most providers send a name, a number, an email and a postcode. We send you buying signals.
Expect your first lead within 24-48 hours of payment confirmation. It can occasionally take longer, as every lead is generated in real time and we wait on genuine demand in your area. After that, leads arrive as homeowners enquire, up to the weekly limit you set for each area, and you are only charged for the leads you receive. We work to deliver as fast as we can. How many arrive each week depends on the market in your area: how many homeowners are looking, and how many other installers are advertising to them. Wider service areas (100km+ radius) give us more homeowners to reach, so they usually fill faster. The quality doesn't change at volume. The same verification, the same named consent, the same survey depth. If your geo supports it, we deliver at scale without compromise. See our Pay Per Lead Terms for full details.
