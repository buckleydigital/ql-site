How it's calculated

Three inputs. One ceiling.

01

Job capacity

Each crew can complete roughly 2.5 jobs per week on average. Multiply by your crew count to find your weekly ceiling.

02

Lead volume

Divide your weekly job capacity by your close rate. This tells you exactly how many leads you need to keep every crew fully booked.

03

Revenue ceiling

Max jobs per month multiplied by your average job value. This is the revenue cap your current team can realistically deliver.

The formulas:  Weekly Leads = (Crews × 2.5) ÷ Close Rate   ·   Revenue Ceiling = Crews × 2.5 × 4.33 × Job Value   ·   Based on an industry average of 2.5 jobs per crew per week across trade verticals.
