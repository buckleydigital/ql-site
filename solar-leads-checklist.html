Quote-ready solar leads:
the 6-point checklist.

If most of your leads don't pick up, aren't the homeowner, or just want a price, the problem is usually how they're captured, not who you're targeting. Run your setup against these six.

Solar Lead Gen 2 min read
The checklist

Six things that decide if a lead books.

  1. Ads go to a page, not an instant form.

    An instant form is one tap, filled in from their Facebook profile. People submit it while scrolling and forget they did. Send the click to a landing page you control.

  2. The survey asks what a quote needs.

    Eight quick tap questions, contact details last:

    Solar, battery or bothExisting systemSystem ageFeed-in tariffQuarterly billOwns the homeProperty typeTimeframe

  3. People you can't sell to are screened out.

    Renters, "just researching" and out-of-area enquiries are stopped before they reach you, not after you've called them.

  4. Meta knows which leads were good.

    The Pixel and Conversions API fire only on a finished survey. When a lead books or signs, that goes back to Meta too, so it finds more people like them.

  5. Every lead hears from you within 5 minutes.

    An automatic text within seconds, then a call while they're still thinking about it. A quote-ready lead is probably filling in a competitor's form too.

  6. You track cost per booked job, not cost per lead.

    Ad spend ÷ jobs booked. A $30 lead that books 1 in 15 costs $450 a job. A $100 lead that books 1 in 3 costs $300, with a fifth of the phone calls.

Ticked fewer than four? That's where your lead quality is going. Each one you fix shows up as fewer dead calls, not just cheaper leads.
