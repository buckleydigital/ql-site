Overview

Program agreement.

This Growth Accelerator Program Agreement ("Agreement") becomes active upon successful completion of registration and onboarding. By participating in the QuoteLeads Growth Accelerator Program ("Program"), you ("Client") agree to be bound by these terms.

This Agreement operates alongside our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to these terms in their entirety, please do not enroll in the Program.

Section 01

Services provided.

QuoteLeads agrees to provide a done-for-you lead generation system to generate leads for the Client's business via the management of paid advertising channels under the Client's branding. During the term of this Agreement, QuoteLeads shall perform the following services:

  • Marketing Channels Managed: Manage and optimize the Client's paid advertising channels
  • Streamlined CRM: Set up and manage a streamlined CRM system for tracking leads
  • Tailored Touchpoints: Implement customized communication strategies to engage leads
  • Dedicated Growth Management: Provide support relating to lead generation, marketing systems, and business growth
  • Automation & AI-Assisted Tools: Utilize automation and AI-assisted tools to support lead handling and follow-up processes where appropriate
  • Tracking & Analytics: Provide tracking and performance insights for campaigns
No Long-Term Lock-In: This Agreement operates on a month-to-month basis following the initial 30-day term.
Section 02

No revenue guarantee.

QuoteLeads does not guarantee specific revenue, sales, or profit outcomes. Results depend on multiple factors outside QuoteLeads' control, including but not limited to:

  • The Client's pricing structure and competitive positioning
  • The Client's sales process and conversion capabilities
  • Responsiveness to leads and follow-up speed
  • Current market conditions and seasonal demand
  • Operational capacity to fulfill new business
  • Local competition and territory dynamics
Section 03

30-day satisfaction guarantee.

If QuoteLeads fails to deploy and operate the lead generation system in accordance with this Agreement, and the Client has complied with all obligations herein, the Client may qualify for a refund of the upfront set-up fee if dissatisfied within the initial 30-day management period.

Important: Refunds apply only to fees paid to QuoteLeads and do not include advertising spend paid to third-party platforms. Advertising spend paid to external platforms is non-refundable.
Section 04

Qualification criteria for the 30-day guarantee.

To qualify for a refund, all conditions below must be met:

1. Advertising Budget

  • The Client must commit to spending the agreed advertising budget for a minimum of 30 days
  • The Client must adhere to QuoteLeads' recommended budget aligned with campaign objectives

2. Follow-Up and Engagement

  • The Client must demonstrate clear, documented efforts to follow up and engage with leads promptly
  • Final conversion outcomes are dependent on the Client's sales and fulfilment process

3. Performance Evaluation

  • Maintain records of follow-ups and interactions in the CRM or tracking system provided
  • Notify QuoteLeads of required adjustments in strategy in a timely manner

4. CRM Usage

  • Provide access to an existing CRM or actively use the CRM supplied by QuoteLeads

5. Extended Pauses

  • A pause longer than 3 consecutive days or more than 7 cumulative days within the 30-day period disqualifies the Client from the guarantee

6. Excessive Requests

  • Requests exceeding one campaign or creative change per week, or those contrary to QuoteLeads' recommendations, may disqualify the Client from the guarantee
Section 05

Responsibilities.

QuoteLeads Responsibilities

  • Build, deploy, manage, and optimize the lead generation system
  • Deliver leads into the Client's CRM or agreed system
  • Provide operational and system-level support

Client Responsibilities

  • Pay the set-up fee
  • Grant access to required advertising accounts
  • Use reasonable efforts to engage and follow up with leads
  • Notify QuoteLeads of any operational or strategic changes impacting campaigns
Section 06

Communication method.

Primary: WhatsApp

Secondary: Email or phone if WhatsApp is unavailable

Section 07

Fees & billing.

Invoice Amount

Fees payable as stated on QuoteLeads' invoice.

Additional Charges

  • Clients are billed monthly for optional SMS credits used inside QuoteLeads CRM and additional services as outlined in proposals
  • Clients pay ad spend directly to advertising platforms
  • QuoteLeads covers the first 50 leads or 500 SMS sent per 30-day period (whichever comes first)
  • Thereafter, SMS is billed at $0.10 per message

Billing and Payment Terms

All fees are payable upon receipt of invoice unless otherwise stated.

Changes to Fees

Any changes will be communicated in advance and applied from the next billing cycle.

Section 08

Conditions for refund.

Refunds are only applicable where:

  • The Client meets all qualification criteria outlined in Section 04
  • Written notice of dissatisfaction is provided within the initial 30-day period
Refunds outside these conditions are issued solely at the discretion of QuoteLeads and do not constitute an admission of fault or an ongoing obligation. Advertising spend paid to external platforms is non-refundable.
Section 09

Term & termination.

This Agreement commences upon onboarding and remains in effect for 30 days. Thereafter, it continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with written notice.

Confidentiality, IP, and limitation clauses survive termination.

Section 10

Intellectual property.

Upon full payment, the Client owns all advertising accounts, creatives, and data generated under their brand.

QuoteLeads retains ownership of all proprietary systems, templates, processes, frameworks, and methodologies used in delivering the services.

Section 11

Confidentiality.

Both parties agree to keep confidential all non-public information shared during the term of this Agreement, including business strategies, data, and system designs.

Section 12

Limitation of liability.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, QuoteLeads' total liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid by the Client to QuoteLeads in the preceding 30 days.

QuoteLeads shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses including lost profits or revenue.

Section 13

Governing law.

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Australia, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of Australian courts.
