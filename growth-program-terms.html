Program agreement.

This Agreement operates alongside our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to these terms in their entirety, please do not enrol in the Program.

Section 01

Services provided.

QuoteLeads agrees to provide a done-for-you lead generation system to generate leads for the Client's business via the management of paid advertising channels under the Client's branding. During the term of this Agreement, QuoteLeads shall perform the following services:

  • Marketing Channels Managed: Manage and optimise the Client's paid advertising channels
  • Streamlined CRM: Set up and manage a streamlined CRM system for tracking leads
  • Tailored Touchpoints: Implement customised communication strategies to engage leads
  • Dedicated Growth Management: Provide support relating to lead generation, marketing systems, and business growth
  • Automation & AI-Assisted Tools: Utilise automation and AI-assisted tools to support lead handling and follow-up processes where appropriate
  • Tracking & Analytics: Provide tracking and performance insights for campaigns
No Long-Term Lock-In: This Agreement operates on a month-to-month basis following the initial 30-day term.
Section 02

No revenue guarantee.

QuoteLeads does not guarantee specific revenue, sales, or profit outcomes. Results depend on multiple factors outside QuoteLeads' control, including but not limited to:

  • The Client's pricing structure and competitive positioning
  • The Client's sales process and conversion capabilities
  • Responsiveness to leads and follow-up speed
  • Current market conditions and seasonal demand
  • Operational capacity to fulfil new business
  • Local competition and territory dynamics
Section 03

30-day service delivery guarantee.

QuoteLeads commits to building and delivering the lead generation system described in Section 01 for every Client who enrols. The build will be delivered within 7 business days of QuoteLeads receiving all required account access and credentials from the Client.

If QuoteLeads fails to deliver the build within that timeframe, and the failure is not caused or contributed to by the Client (including any delay in providing required access, approvals, or information), the Client is entitled to a full refund of the set-up fee upon written request.

Once the build has been delivered, the set-up fee is fully earned and non-refundable. No refund can be claimed on any basis after delivery is confirmed, including dissatisfaction with results, lead quality, or campaign performance.

Definition of "Delivered"

Delivery is confirmed when all three of the following are complete and a preview has been sent to the Client:

  • Ad creatives and campaign structure have been built and a preview has been sent to the Client
  • The CRM has been configured and is ready to receive leads
  • At least one creative set has been completed and submitted to the Client for approval

Delivery is confirmed at the point the preview is sent, regardless of whether campaigns have subsequently gone live.

The 30-Day Management Period

The 30-day management period is separate from and in addition to the build and set-up phase. The management period begins once the system has been delivered as defined above, and consists of 30 days during which the Client's advertising campaigns are live and actively running.

Where ads are paused for 3 consecutive days or fewer — for any reason, including billing issues or circumstances outside either party's control — those days are paused and do not count toward the 30-day management period, but no credit is issued. Where ads are paused for more than 3 consecutive days, those excess pause days are credited toward the Client's next billing cycle. In all cases, the current billing cycle is billed in full at the end of the management period as agreed by both parties.

Important: Refunds apply only to fees paid directly to QuoteLeads. Advertising spend paid to third-party platforms is non-refundable under any circumstances.
Section 04

Qualification criteria for the 30-day service delivery guarantee.

To qualify for a refund, all conditions below must be met:

1. Advertising Budget

  • The Client must commit to spending the agreed advertising budget for a minimum of 30 days
  • The Client must adhere to QuoteLeads' recommended budget aligned with campaign objectives

2. Follow-Up and Engagement

The Client must make a minimum of 3 documented contact attempts per lead within 48 hours of delivery, logged inside the CRM provided by QuoteLeads. Failure to meet this standard disqualifies the Client from the service delivery guarantee. Undocumented follow-up attempts will not be considered.

3. Performance Evaluation

  • Maintain records of follow-ups and interactions in the CRM or tracking system provided
  • Notify QuoteLeads of required adjustments in strategy in a timely manner

4. CRM Usage

  • Provide access to an existing CRM or actively use the CRM supplied by QuoteLeads

5. Extended Pauses

A pause is defined as any period during which the Client's advertising campaigns are paused, deactivated, or suspended for any reason, including but not limited to: budget withdrawal, account issues, billing-related suspensions, platform-imposed restrictions, or circumstances outside either party's control. Communication gaps or periods of Client unresponsiveness do not constitute a pause unless accompanied by campaign deactivation. Where ads are paused for 3 consecutive days or fewer, those days do not count toward the 30-day management period and no credit is issued. Where ads are paused for more than 3 consecutive days, the excess pause days are credited toward the Client's next billing cycle. In all cases, the current billing cycle remains payable in full at the end of the management period. The service delivery guarantee applies only to days on which campaigns are live and actively running.

6. Excessive Requests

  • Requests exceeding one campaign or creative change per week, or those contrary to QuoteLeads' recommendations, may disqualify the Client from the guarantee
Section 05

Responsibilities.

QuoteLeads Responsibilities

  • Build, deploy, manage, and optimise the lead generation system
  • Deliver leads into the Client's CRM or agreed system
  • Provide operational and system-level support

Client Responsibilities

  • Pay the set-up fee
  • Grant access to required advertising accounts
  • Use reasonable efforts to engage and follow up with leads
  • Notify QuoteLeads of any operational or strategic changes impacting campaigns
Section 06

Client interference & platform compliance.

QuoteLeads accepts no liability for campaign performance disrupted by the Client's direct interference with advertising accounts, including but not limited to unauthorised campaign modifications, budget changes made without QuoteLeads' approval, or actions that result in ad account suspension or platform policy violations.

If Client interference causes measurable disruption to campaign delivery, QuoteLeads' performance obligations are suspended for the affected period and the service delivery guarantee is void for that period.

The Client is responsible for any costs or penalties arising from their own platform policy violations.

Section 08

Fees & billing.

Invoice Amount

Fees payable as stated on QuoteLeads' invoice.

Additional Charges

  • Clients are billed monthly for optional SMS credits used inside QuoteLeads CRM and additional services as outlined in proposals
  • Clients pay ad spend directly to advertising platforms
  • QuoteLeads covers the first 50 leads or 500 SMS sent per billing cycle (whichever comes first). This inclusion resets on the same date each month as the Client's invoice date. All usage is measured from invoice date to invoice date. Unused inclusions do not roll over.
  • Thereafter, SMS is billed at $0.10 per message

Billing and Payment Terms

Invoices are due upon receipt. If payment is not received within 5 calendar days of the invoice date, QuoteLeads reserves the right to suspend all services until the outstanding balance is cleared. Days during which services are suspended due to non-payment do not count toward the 30-day management period and do not extend any guarantee obligations.

GST

Unless otherwise stated, all fees quoted are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). Where GST applies, it will be added to the invoice amount at the applicable rate. QuoteLeads will issue a valid tax invoice in accordance with the requirements of the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth).

Changes to Fees

Any changes to ongoing management fees will be communicated in writing with a minimum of 14 days' notice and will take effect from the next billing cycle. Continued use of the services following notification constitutes acceptance of the revised fees.

Section 09

Conditions for refund.

The set-up fee is refundable only where QuoteLeads has failed to deliver the build within 7 business days of receiving all required account access from the Client, as defined in Section 03. No refund will be issued on the basis of dissatisfaction with results, lead quality, lead volume, campaign performance, or any outcome-based expectation.

A refund request will only be considered where all of the following conditions are met:

  • The build has not been delivered (no preview sent, no deliverables produced) within 7 business days of QuoteLeads receiving all required account access
  • The failure to deliver is not caused or contributed to by the Client, including any delay in providing required access, approvals, or information
  • Written notice of the refund request is submitted to QuoteLeads within the initial 30-day management period
  • The Client has complied with all obligations under this Agreement
Any refund issued outside these conditions is made solely at the discretion of QuoteLeads, does not constitute an admission of fault, and creates no ongoing obligation. Advertising spend paid to external platforms is non-refundable under any circumstances.
Section 10

Term & termination.

The 30-day initial term does not commence until QuoteLeads has received all required account access and credentials from the Client. Any delay by the Client in providing access does not count toward the 30-day period. The commencement date will be confirmed in writing by QuoteLeads once all access has been received.

Thereafter, this Agreement continues on a month-to-month basis. The Client may cancel at any time without notice and without penalty. QuoteLeads may terminate this Agreement with a minimum of 30 days' written notice to the Client, given in accordance with Section 18 of this Agreement.

All fees accrued up to the date of termination remain due and payable. Where the Client terminates prior to the end of a billing cycle, fees for the full current billing cycle remain payable and will not be pro-rated.

QuoteLeads may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the Client: (a) fails to pay any amount due within 10 days of the due date; (b) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy the breach within 7 days of written notice; or (c) becomes insolvent, enters administration, or is wound up.

Sections 11 (Intellectual Property), 12 (Confidentiality), 13 (Limitation of Liability), and 18 (General Provisions) survive termination of this Agreement.

This applies in the event of non-payment, early termination by the Client, or any unresolved outstanding balance.

Section 16

Force majeure.

Neither party will be in breach of this Agreement or liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations to the extent that such delay or failure is caused by a Force Majeure Event.

A Force Majeure Event means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected party, including but not limited to: acts of God, natural disasters, pandemic, epidemic, war, terrorism, government restrictions, civil unrest, power outages, internet or telecommunications failures, or changes to third-party platform policies (including advertising platform policy changes, account suspensions imposed by platforms, or algorithm changes).

The affected party must notify the other party as soon as practicable of the Force Majeure Event and take reasonable steps to minimise the impact. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 30 days, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice without liability, subject to payment of fees accrued to the date of termination.

Days affected by a Force Majeure Event do not count toward the 30-day management period and will be credited toward the Client's next billing cycle in accordance with Section 03.

Section 17

Dispute resolution.

The parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement through the following process before commencing legal proceedings (other than applications for urgent interlocutory relief):

Step 1 — Written Notice: The party raising the dispute must give written notice to the other party specifying the nature of the dispute and the resolution sought.

Step 2 — Negotiation: Within 10 business days of the notice, representatives of both parties must meet (in person, by telephone, or video) and attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith.

Step 3 — Mediation: If the dispute is not resolved within 20 business days of the written notice, either party may refer the matter to mediation with a mutually agreed mediator. The cost of mediation shall be shared equally between the parties unless otherwise agreed.

Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking urgent injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.
