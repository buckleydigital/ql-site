The fine print, in plain English

Every condition. Up front.

  • $100 a day minimum ad spend ($3,000 a month) on your own Meta ad account to qualify.
  • Averaged across your first 30 days live. Total ad spend Meta bills you, divided by the quote-ready enquiries it produced.
  • If the average is over $100, your monthly management fee is waived and we keep working until a 30-day window lands under $100. Ad spend is always yours to pay, direct to Meta.
  • Log your call attempts in QuoteLeadsHQ. If a lead has not had three logged attempts within 48 hours, it counts as quote-ready.
  • Approve our creative change requests within 24 hours. When results dip, new ads are how we fix it fast. A request left waiting longer pauses the guarantee clock until you reply.
  • Keep the campaign running as agreed. The guarantee pauses if the ads are paused, the budget drops below $100 a day, the service area shrinks, or the creative or offer is swapped for one we did not build.
