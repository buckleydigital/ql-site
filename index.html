FAQ

Common Questions

Do I have to use your CRM, or can you work with my existing tools? +

You have three options. Full Platform: Use QuoteLeads as your complete sales dashboard with pipeline, SMS, and AI automation. Pass-Through: We qualify leads and send them straight to your email, phone, or existing tools via API. Custom Integration: Enterprise setup where leads flow into your specific workflow (ServiceTitan, Salesforce, etc.) with custom tagging and routing. Most trades start with Pass-Through, then graduate to Full Platform once they see the conversion lift.

What if I already run my own ads? +

Perfect. We can either take over campaign management for you, or you keep running ads and use QuoteLeads just for the lead handling and conversion infrastructure. Both options give you full system access.

How are leads delivered? +

Directly into your QuoteLeads dashboard in real-time. You'll also get instant notifications via SMS or email. If you choose Pass-Through or Custom Integration, leads flow directly into your existing systems the moment they are qualified.

Can I add my sales team? +

Yes. Add unlimited reps, set up postcode-based routing, round-robin assignment, or manual assignment. Each rep gets their own login and sees only their pipeline.

What trades do you work with? +

We specialize in high-value trade services: renovations, solar, landscaping, painting, roofing, decking and similar. If your average job is $3k+, we can help.

How quickly can I get started? +

Strategy call, campaigns or leads live within 24-48 hours. System is pre-configured on signup.
