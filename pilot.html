Why It's Built This Way

One job covers it.
The rest is upside.

Most lead generation in home services runs on a per-lead model. An agency generates enquiries from the cheapest available source, form fills, aggregated data, recycled contacts, marks them up, and sells them individually. The incentive is volume, not quality. The more leads that move, the more revenue the seller makes, regardless of whether those leads turn into real work.

That model creates a structural problem. When the seller's revenue depends on quantity, there's no economic reason to filter hard, qualify properly, or care whether the homeowner is serious. It's why so many tradies have a drawer full of leads they've paid for and never converted. The leads weren't built to convert. They were built to sell.

We don't sell leads as an ongoing business model. Marking up every enquiry and clipping the ticket on each one doesn't make sense long term for either side - it pressures the agency to cut corners on sourcing, and it bleeds the business buying them. The economics only work when quality stays high and the cost structure is transparent.

That's why we build the system into your business directly. You own the infrastructure, you own the leads, and the cost per enquiry drops the longer it runs. Market Pulse is the proof round - $650 + GST flat, no per-lead markup, no ongoing clip. A way to see the quality of what comes through before any of that is built.
