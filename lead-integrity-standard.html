📍 Postcode-Based Matching

Our matching engine doesn't just assign leads randomly. It uses postcode-level geographic data to connect homeowners with installers who actually service their area.

Every installer on our platform defines their coverage zones, and our system respects those boundaries. You only receive leads from postcodes you've opted into.

The matching logic considers:

1 Geographic Coverage

Leads are matched to installers based on the homeowner's postcode and the installer's defined service area. No out-of-area jobs landing in your inbox.

2 Capacity Caps

Each installer sets a maximum number of leads per period. Once you hit your cap, new leads in your area route to the next available installer. You never get overloaded.

3 Round-Robin Fairness

When multiple installers cover the same postcode, our system distributes leads evenly using a round-robin model. No favourites, no bidding wars — just fair, predictable distribution.

The result: installers receive leads they can actually service, in areas they actually cover, at volumes they can actually handle. And homeowners get matched with someone local who's ready to help.
