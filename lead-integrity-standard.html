Named Buyer Consent

Your business name.
Before they hit submit.

Most lead generation works with vague consent language - "our network of trusted partners" or "selected third parties." The homeowner has no idea who's about to call them. That's how you end up with people who hang up the moment they hear your name.

Every system we build works differently. When a homeowner fills out a quote form, our infrastructure uses their postcode to match them with your business in real time - before they submit their details. Your company name appears in the consent text on the form itself.

One Lead, One Business

Exclusive delivery.
Not a marketplace.

The standard model in lead generation is to sell the same enquiry to three, four, sometimes five businesses. Everyone races to dial first. Whoever picks up fastest wins - not whoever is the best fit for the job.

That's not how systems we build work. Every lead goes to exactly one business - the one the homeowner consented to. It's never shared, never resold, and never recycled into a database for later use.

The result is a fundamentally different sales conversation. You're not competing against four other quotes the homeowner didn't ask for. You're following up on an enquiry they made directly to your business, expecting your call.

Postcode-Level Matching

Your territory.
Your leads.

The matching engine built into your system doesn't assign leads randomly. It uses postcode-level geographic data to connect homeowners with the business that services their area - your business.

You define your coverage zones. The system respects those boundaries. You only receive leads from postcodes you've opted into, at volumes you can handle.

1 Geographic coverage

Leads are matched based on the homeowner's postcode and your defined service area. No out-of-area enquiries landing in your inbox.

2 Capacity controls

You set a maximum lead volume per period. The system respects that cap so you never get overloaded beyond what your team can handle.

3 Territory exclusivity

Full system builds lock your territory exclusively to your business. No competitors running the same system in your area.

The result: you receive leads you can actually service, in areas you actually cover, at volumes your team can actually convert. And homeowners get matched with someone local who's ready to help.
