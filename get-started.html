About the application.

The system we build only performs when the business behind it is ready. A strong team, a proven close rate, and fast response time are the three biggest predictors of success. If those aren't in place, we'd rather tell you now than have you invest in infrastructure that underperforms.
We'll review your application and reach out within one business day to book a 20-minute discovery call. That call is enough to understand whether we're a good fit and what the system would look like for your trade and territory. No pitch deck, no hard sell.
Most businesses invest between $7,000 and $15,000+ to have the system properly built and deployed. The scope varies depending on your trade, territory, and the infrastructure that needs to be put in place. Ad spend is managed separately. We'll recommend a budget during the discovery call.
No lock-in. The implementation fee covers the build. If you choose ongoing management, there's a 30-day notice period to cancel. The infrastructure we build belongs to you - it doesn't disappear if you stop working with us.
In most cases, the first leads arrive within 24–48 hours of the campaign going live. The build phase typically takes 5–7 business days. So from signing on to first lead - you're typically looking at around 7–10 days total.
If your application doesn't meet our criteria, we'll explain why clearly. Most of the time it comes down to capacity, process, or timing. If things change - you bring on a team, sharpen your sales process, or you're genuinely ready to invest - come back and apply again. We mean it.
