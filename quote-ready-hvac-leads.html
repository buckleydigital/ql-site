What we point it at

One system. Three ways to aim it.

Ducted installs

Whole-home systems, the biggest jobs in residential HVAC. Qualified on property size, zones, existing ductwork and timeframe before they reach you.

Split systems

Single and multi-head installs. Quicker jobs you can stack up, qualified on room count, property type and whether it's new or a replacement.

Replacements

Homeowners with an old or failing system who need it swapped. They already know what air conditioning costs, so there is less educating on the call, and they need it done.

Pick one at activation, or run more than one and split the budget. Because the system is yours, you can change what it targets whenever you want, without rebuilding anything.

What to expect

Quality first. Volume second.

These are premium leads, so quality comes before volume. Prefer volume? We can trim the survey questions so more people finish it. How many you get depends on your service area, offer, ad budget and the time of year, and we'll give you a realistic estimate for your area on the call.

What counts as a quote-ready lead

A homeowner in your service area who came through your funnel, gave their name, phone, email and postcode, and answered every question on our standard 8-question HVAC survey:

  1. What they need: split system, ducted or a replacement
  2. Whether it's a new install or replacing an old system
  3. How many rooms or zones
  4. What type of property it is, and its size
  5. Whether there's existing ductwork (for ducted)
  6. Their budget range
  7. Whether they own the home (tenants are screened out)
  8. When they want it installed

Want different questions? Swap any of them for what matters to your business at onboarding.
