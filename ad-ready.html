Questions

Answered.

I'm new and haven't done any jobs yet. Is this still for me?

Yes. Everything is set up the same way. The review campaign needs past customers, so if you have none yet we leave it out.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. Every review comes from a real customer of yours. We only send the request; we never write, buy, incentivise or filter reviews, which is also what Google requires.

I already have a website. Do I still need this?

We start by checking what you have. If your site is solid we optimise it, otherwise we build a new one. Either way you end up ad-ready.

Are there any ongoing fees?

No monthly fees. Website hosting is $0 a month, for life. The only running cost is your .com.au domain, around $20 a year, registered in your business name. No hidden fees.

Will I rank number one on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a ranking. We set up everything Google looks at for local businesses, so you are in the best position to rank.

What is not included?

Ad spend and running ads. When you are ready, upgrade to a Standard or Growth ads build within 60 days and $250 comes off the price.

Who owns everything?

You do. Your domain, website content, Google profile and listings are in your business name.
