Overview

What these Terms cover.

These Platform Access Terms ("Terms") govern your access to and use of the QuoteLeads Growth Accelerator platform ("Platform"). Section 1

Platform Features.

Upon activation, the Platform provides:

  • Paid advertising campaign build and deployment under the Client's branding
  • CRM configuration and lead delivery infrastructure
  • Customised communication and follow-up automations
  • Full access to QuoteLeads HQ for campaign visibility, lead tracking, and performance data
  • AI-assisted lead follow-up tools and automation workflows
  • Tracking, analytics, and campaign performance reporting

Platform access continues on a month-to-month basis following the initial term. The Client may cancel at any time without notice or penalty.

QuoteLeads does not guarantee specific revenue, sales, lead volume, or profit outcomes. Section 3

Platform Build & Delivery.

QuoteLeads will build and configure the Client's platform system following receipt of all required account access and credentials. The build window is up to 7 business days from that date.

In practice, QuoteLeads aims to have initial advertising previews delivered to the Client within 24-48 hours of receiving all required access, with systems fully configured and ready to go live within 3-5 business days.

Definition of delivery: Platform delivery is confirmed at the point QuoteLeads sends the Client their initial advertising previews. This constitutes delivery of the built system - the platform is configured, the CRM is ready to receive leads, and the advertising infrastructure is complete and ready to activate.

Once initial advertising previews are provided to the Client, the platform build is considered complete and the build fee is fully earned. No refund of the build fee will be issued under any circumstances after this point, regardless of whether the Client proceeds to activation, changes their circumstances, or is dissatisfied with any aspect of the output.

Following delivery, the Client's platform enters a 30-day management and optimisation window. During this period, QuoteLeads monitors platform performance, refines campaign delivery, and makes optimisation adjustments at no additional cost. This period begins on the date the Client's ads first go live. Any pausing of ads during this period is the Client's responsibility and does not extend or reset the window.

Section 4

Build Fee - Non-Refundable Upon Delivery.

The build fee is non-refundable in all circumstances once initial advertising previews have been provided by QuoteLeads. This applies regardless of the Client's decision to activate the system, changes in business circumstances, dissatisfaction with creative output, or any other reason.

The sole exception is where QuoteLeads has failed to deliver initial advertising previews within 7 business days of receiving all required account access from the Client, and that failure is not caused or contributed to by the Client (including any delay in providing required credentials, approvals, or information). In that event only, a refund of the build fee may be requested in writing.

A refund will only be considered where all of the following conditions are met:

  • No advertising previews or build deliverables have been provided within 7 business days of QuoteLeads receiving all required access
  • The delay is entirely attributable to QuoteLeads and not caused or contributed to by the Client
  • A written refund request is submitted within the initial 30-day window
  • The Client has met all obligations under these Terms

Advertising spend paid directly to third-party platforms is non-refundable under any circumstances.

Section 6

Qualification Criteria (30-Day Management Window).

To remain eligible for the build delivery guarantee and the included 30-day management window, all of the following must be maintained:

  1. Advertising budget: The Client must maintain the agreed advertising budget while campaigns are active and follow QuoteLeads' recommended budget settings.
  2. Lead follow-up: The Client must make a minimum of 3 documented contact attempts per lead within 48 hours of delivery, logged inside the QuoteLeads CRM. Undocumented attempts will not be counted.
  3. CRM use: The Client must actively use the CRM supplied by QuoteLeads or provide access to their own.
  4. No unauthorised campaign changes: Campaign modifications, budget changes, or other account-level actions made by the Client without QuoteLeads' approval will void the guarantee for the affected period.
  5. Campaign change frequency: Requests exceeding one campaign or creative change per week, or those contrary to QuoteLeads' recommendations, will disqualify the Client from the guarantee.
