What we point it at

One system. Three ways to aim it.

Solar + battery

Homeowners buying panels and storage together as one system. Bigger job values than solar alone, and they are shopping for a complete setup rather than a price per kW.

Battery retrofit

Homes that already have panels and want storage added. They have bought from an installer before, so there is far less educating to do on the call.

Commercial solar

Facility managers, property owners and decision makers enquiring for a business site. Fewer enquiries, far larger jobs, qualified on decision-making authority.

Pick one at activation, or run more than one and split the budget. Because the system is yours, you can change what it targets whenever you want, without rebuilding anything.

What counts as a quote-ready lead

A homeowner in your service area who came through your funnel, gave their name, phone, email and postcode, and answered every question on our standard 8-question solar survey:

  1. What they're interested in: solar, battery or both
  2. Whether they already have solar, and if it has a battery
  3. How old their system is (if they have one)
  4. Their feed-in tariff (if they have one)
  5. Their average quarterly power bill
  6. Whether they own the home (renters are screened out)
  7. What type of property it is
  8. When they're looking to install

Some leads upload their power bill too, as an optional extra. Want different questions? Swap any of them for what matters to your business at onboarding.
