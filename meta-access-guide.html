Meta Business Access Guide

How to grant partner access to your Meta Page, Ad Account and Pixel - takes about 5 minutes.

⚡

Why we set it up this way

By connecting your Page, Ad Account and Pixel under your own Meta Business portfolio, you own every asset from day one. This avoids complicated transfer processes if you ever change agencies.

1
Open Meta Business Settings

Go to business.facebook.com/settings and log in with your Facebook account.

If you see "Select business" with no options, click Create Business to set up your Business Portfolio first.

2
Navigate to Partners

In the left sidebar click Settings (gear icon), then under Users select Partners.

3
Add QuoteLeads as a Partner

Click the Add button (top right). Select "Give a partner access to your assets".

4
Enter QuoteLeads' Business ID

Paste the Business ID into the dialog field, then click Next.

5
Assign Your Facebook Page

Select your Page from the dropdown, then set permissions as shown below.

Don't miss the Leads toggle

The Leads permission is required for us to deliver your leads. If it is left OFF, enquiries from your campaigns cannot reach you. Please double check it is switched ON before you click Save.

Permission Status Description
Content ON Create, manage or delete posts, stories and more as the Page
Community activity ON Review and respond to comments, remove unwanted content
Messages and calls OFF Send and respond to messages and calls as the Page
Ads ON Create, manage and delete ads for the Page
Insights ON See how the Page, content and ads perform
Revenue ON View and export Page monetisation and earnings data
Leads ON (required) Required to deliver your leads. Access and manage leads, including downloading leads

Already saved with Leads OFF? Here's how to fix it

  1. Go to Settings → Users → Partners and click QuoteLeads.
  2. Under the shared assets, select your Page and choose to edit or manage its permissions.
  3. Switch Leads ON and click Save. No need to start again.
6
Assign Your Ad Account

Select your Ad Account from the dropdown. Enable Full control - Manage ad accounts. Before saving, confirm the Leads toggle on your Page is ON, then click Save.

Permission Status Description
Manage campaigns (ads) OFF Create and edit ads, access reports and view ads
View performance OFF Access reports and view ads
Manage Creative Hub mockups OFF View, create and edit mockups in Creative Hub
Manage ad accounts ON ✓ Full control - Control ad account settings, finances and permissions
7
Go to Datasets and pixels

Still in Business Settings, open Data Sources in the left sidebar and select Datasets and pixels.

Your Pixel is required

Your Pixel (called a Dataset in Meta) tells Meta which ads are turning into real enquiries, so it can find you more of them. We cannot launch your campaigns without it. The Pixel stays owned by your business.

8
Add Your Pixel (Dataset)

Click Add. Give your dataset a name (for example, your business name) and click Create.

Already have a Pixel? Skip creating a new one and simply select your existing dataset from the list.

9
Share Your Pixel with QuoteLeads
  1. With your dataset selected, click Assign partners.
  2. Choose Business ID and paste the ID below.
  3. Turn on Full control (Manage dataset). Partial access is not enough for us to set up your tracking.
  4. Click Assign.

10
Confirm - You're Done!

Open Settings → Users → Partners → QuoteLeads and tick off each item below. Leads and the Pixel are the two most often missed, so please check them before you finish.
